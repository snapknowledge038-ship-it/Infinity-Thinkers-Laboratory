DINOSAURS

The Era of Giant Reptiles

The Age of Giants

Dinosaurs ruled the Earth for over 160 million years, from the Triassic period until their sudden extinction at the end of the Cretaceous. They were an incredibly diverse group, ranging from the size of a chicken to massive long-necked titans as heavy as space rockets.

Tyrannosaurus Rex

The "King of the Tyrant Lizards." With one of the strongest bite forces of any land animal, the T-Rex was a apex predator of North America.

Diet: Carnivore (Meat-eater)
Length: 12 - 13 Meters
Era: Late Cretaceous

Triceratops

Known for its three horns and massive bony frill. This armored herbivore was likely a formidable opponent even for the T-Rex.

Diet: Herbivore (Plant-eater)
Weight: 6,000 - 12,000 kg
Feature: Defensive Horns

Brachiosaurus

A gentle giant with an incredibly long neck, allowing it to reach the highest leaves in Jurassic forests. It walked on all fours like a giraffe.

Diet: Herbivore
Height: 12 - 16 Meters
Era: Late Jurassic

Velociraptor

Small but deadly. Unlike movies, these were feathered hunters, roughly the size of a turkey, known for their sharp sickle-shaped toe claws.

Diet: Carnivore
Speed: Up to 40 mph
Feature: High Intelligence

Spinosaurus

The largest of all carnivorous dinosaurs, even bigger than T-Rex. It had a massive sail on its back and lived both on land and in water.

Diet: Carnivore (Piscivore)
Environment: River Systems
Era: Cretaceous
