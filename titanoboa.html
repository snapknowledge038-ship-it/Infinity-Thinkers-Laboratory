TITANOBOA

The 42-Foot Prehistoric Serpent

The Jungle Terror

After the extinction of the dinosaurs, a new king emerged in the tropical swamps of South America. Titanoboa cerrejonensis was a massive snake that lived about 60 to 58 million years ago. It didn't use venom to kill; instead, it used its immense weight and strength to crush its prey to death.

12.8 Meters

Quick Facts

Giant Diet: Titanoboa was so large that it could easily swallow giant turtles and prehistoric crocodiles whole.
Warm Climate: They required a very hot environment to survive. It is estimated that the tropics during their time were much hotter than they are today.
Modern Relatives: It is most closely related to modern-day Boas and Anacondas, but many times larger.
Discovery: Its fossils were first discovered in 2009 in a coal mine in Colombia.
