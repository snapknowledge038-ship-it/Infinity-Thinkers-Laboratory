SMILODON

The Ultimate Ice Age Predator

The Sabre-Toothed Cat

The Smilodon is one of the most famous prehistoric mammals. Often called the "Sabre-toothed Tiger," it was not actually a tiger but a distant cousin. Living during the Pleistocene epoch, it was built for power rather than speed, capable of taking down massive prey like bison and small mammoths.

Fearful Facts

  • Deadly Teeth: Their iconic 11-inch long canine teeth were fragile. They only used them to deliver a finishing bite to the throat after pinning down prey.
  • Social Hunters: Evidence suggests Smilodon lived and hunted in packs, much like modern lions.
  • Sturdy Build: They had extremely muscular front legs to wrestle large animals to the ground.
