THE GREAT EMU WAR

Birds vs Machine Guns

In 1932, Australia declared war on a flightless bird. Over 20,000 Emus invaded farmland in Western Australia, causing massive destruction to crops. The government responded by sending the Seventh Heavy Battery of the Royal Australian Artillery.

The Tactics

  • The Weaponry: Soldiers were armed with Lewis machine guns and 10,000 rounds of ammunition to fight the birds.
  • Emu Guerilla Warfare: The birds proved to be incredibly fast and smart, splitting into small groups to avoid being shot.
  • The Failure: Despite the firepower, the army failed to significantly reduce the emu population.

A Weird Victory

The war ended in a "defeat" for the humans. Major Meredith, the commander, famously compared the emus to Zulus, stating, "If we had a military division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds, it would face any army in the world."
