MEGALODON

The Largest Shark to Ever Exist

The King of the Abyss

Approximately 23 to 3.6 million years ago, the oceans were ruled by a titan. The Otodus megalodon was not just a big shark; it was a massive predator that made the modern Great White shark look like a minnow. Its name literally means "Big Tooth," a fitting title for a creature with teeth the size of a human hand.

Unbelievable Facts

  • Whale Hunters: Megalodons primarily fed on small to medium-sized whales. Their bite was strong enough to crush a whale's skull or vertebrae.
  • Giant Teeth: A single Megalodon tooth could measure over 7 inches (18 cm) in length. They had roughly 276 teeth arranged in five rows.
  • Global Presence: Fossils have been found on every continent except Antarctica, suggesting they lived in warm coastal waters all around the globe.
  • The Extinction: They went extinct around 3.6 million years ago, likely due to global cooling and the disappearance of their primary prey (small whales).
