Our Cosmic Address

A visual journey from our home planet to the infinite reaches of the Multiverse.

1. Earth (The Planet)

Our home and the only place in the known universe where life exists.

Specialty: One of the four terrestrial planets in our Solar System.

Connection: Earth is the third planet orbiting the Sun.

2. Solar System

A star-centered system bound by gravity, including 8 planets and various celestial bodies.

Specialty: The Sun provides the energy for everything within this system.

Connection: Located in the "Orion Arm" of the Milky Way, 25,000 light-years from the center.

3. Milky Way (The Galaxy)

A barred spiral galaxy containing 100 to 400 billion stars.

Specialty: Home to a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A* at its core.

Connection: A member of the "Local Group," which belongs to the Laniakea Supercluster.

4. Laniakea (Supercluster)

A massive structural web of roughly 100,000 galaxies.

Specialty: "Laniakea" means "Immeasurable Heaven" in Hawaiian. Everything here moves toward the "Great Attractor."

Connection: It is a dense node within the greater Cosmic Web.

5. The Cosmic Web

The largest structure in the universe, made of filaments of dark matter and gas.

Specialty: It acts as the skeleton of the universe, with vast empty spaces known as "Voids."

Connection: This is the fundamental framework of the entire Universe.

6. The Universe

The totality of space, time, matter, and energy.

Specialty: Expanding since the Big Bang 13.8 billion years ago. We study the "Observable Universe."

Connection: In modern theory, our universe may be just one part of a Multiverse.

7. The Multiverse

A theoretical concept of infinite universes with different physical laws.

Specialty: Could contain infinite versions of reality where even gravity behaves differently.

Connection: Our universe is like a single bubble in the vast ocean of the Multiverse.

Hierarchy Summary

  • Multiverse → Contains many Universes
  • Our Universe → Built of the Cosmic Web
  • Cosmic Web → Holds Superclusters like Laniakea
  • Laniakea → Contains 100,000 galaxies like the Milky Way
  • Milky Way → Contains our Solar System
  • Solar System → Earth orbits the Sun
  • Earth → Where we live
