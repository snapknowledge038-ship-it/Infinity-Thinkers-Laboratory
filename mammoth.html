WOOLLY MAMMOTH

The Great Titan of the Ice Age

The Frozen Giant

The Woolly Mammoth is the ultimate symbol of the Ice Age. Covered in thick, shaggy fur and possessing massive curved tusks, these cousins of modern elephants roamed the freezing steppes of the Northern Hemisphere for hundreds of thousands of years.

Survival Adaptations

To survive in temperatures as low as -50°C, the Mammoth had several unique features:

  • Three Layers of Fur: A coarse outer layer and a soft, woolly undercoat for insulation.
  • Curved Tusks: Used to sweep snow away from the ground to find hidden grass and plants.
  • Small Ears: Unlike modern African elephants, Mammoths had small ears to prevent heat loss and frostbite.
